# Section 7446: Times and places of sessions

## Metadata
- **Subtitle**: 
- **Chapter**: 
- **Cross-references**: None

## Content

The times and places of the sessions of the Tax Court and of its divisions shall be prescribed by the chief judge with a view to securing reasonable opportunity to taxpayers to appear before the Tax Court or any of its divisions, with as little inconvenience and expense to taxpayers as is practicable. ( Aug. 16, 1954, ch. 736 , 68A Stat. 880 .)
